# Lab 08: Vision Transformer and VLM Results

This folder contains visualizations and experimental outputs from my work with Vision Transformers and Vision Language Models using SmolVLM.

## Results Included

1. **Patch Grid Comparison:** A visualization comparing 48-pixel and 96-pixel image patches.
2. **Chart Reading Results:** A test of the model's ability to extract and interpret information from a sales chart.
3. **Hallucination Test:** An example of the model generating an unsupported explanation in response to a misleading question.
4. **Receipt Reading Results:** An experiment testing the smaller SmolVLM model's ability to extract prices from a custom receipt.

## Key Findings

The experiments demonstrated that VLMs can recognize objects, answer visual questions, and extract numerical information, but their reliability varies depending on the task and prompt.

The chart-reading and hallucination experiments highlighted the importance of verifying model responses against known information. The receipt-reading experiment showed that even a smaller model could successfully identify prices from a clear image.
